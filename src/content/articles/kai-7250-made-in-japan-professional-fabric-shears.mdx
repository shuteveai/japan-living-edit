## 1. Why Fabric Scissors Are a Different Kind of Tool

A general pair of scissors can handle many small cutting tasks.

Fabric asks for a more specific tool because the material itself behaves differently from paper or packaging. It can stretch, slide, bunch, and resist a cut if the tool, surface, or technique does not match the task.

That does not mean every person needs professional fabric shears.

Someone who cuts fabric once or twice a year may not need a dedicated model. Someone who sews, alters clothing, cuts patterns, handles textiles for work, or regularly prepares material for craft projects may care more about blade length, hand fit, pivot behavior, and the way the tool is intended to be maintained.

The point is not to rank fabric scissors against ordinary scissors.

It is to begin with the task.

KAI Europe describes the 7250 as full tang.

In product-design terms, that means the metal structure extends through the handle area rather than ending only at the blade. It is a construction detail, not just a styling detail.

For shears, construction matters because the tool is held, opened, closed, and pressed through material repeatedly. A full-tang design is therefore relevant to how the product is put together.

That does not make an open-ended durability claim.
